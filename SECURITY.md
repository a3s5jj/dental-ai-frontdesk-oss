# Security and privacy

Use synthetic clinic and patient details in issues and pull requests. Do not post
conversation exports, health concerns, names, phone numbers, appointment records,
screenshots of real accounts, or filled configuration files.

Keep credentials in n8n's credential store. Keep `.env`, private clinic profiles,
execution data, database files, and knowledge-base contents outside Git.

This template can process health-related and identifying information. Deployment
requires a jurisdiction-specific privacy, retention, access-control, and incident
response review.
